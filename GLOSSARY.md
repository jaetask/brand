# Glossary

The words Brand uses. Code, tests, docs and conversation use these words and no synonyms.
Brand is built on [open-agent-kit](https://github.com/jaetask/open-agent-kit); the Kit's words (App, Domain, Action, Effect, Call, Outcome, Card, Contract, Connection) keep the meaning its glossary gives them.

## Language

**Brand**: One identity you build and maintain, such as a person or a product. Everything else here belongs to one brand.
_Avoid_: entity, company, client

**Profile**: Who a brand is: its name, picture, purpose, goals and intended audiences.
_Avoid_: about, bio

**Line**: A short, quotable sentence that defines a brand's character, with its credit. Lines are suggested, accepted, rejected or retired.
_Avoid_: identity statement, slogan, tagline

**Guidance**: A brand's voice, look, principles, conduct, audience promise and plan, saved as numbered versions. Every save is a new version.
_Avoid_: settings, config

**Voice**: How a brand sounds: key phrases, borrowed phrases with their credit, tone and an example.
_Avoid_: style guide, tone of voice

**Look**: How a brand looks: its colours, typography and logos.
_Avoid_: visual identity, branding

**Plan**: hoeem's five steps a brand runs by: Proof, Your person, Association, Product and Belt.
_Avoid_: strategy, roadmap

**Step**: One of the plan's five parts. A step is done when every check in "Done when" passes.
_Avoid_: level, stage, phase

**Proof**: Something a brand can show: a story, opinion, fact or reference, with where it came from. It counts for step 1 when it is firsthand and has a link anyone can open.
_Avoid_: knowledge, evidence, content

**Season**: 90 days of the plan, with its dates, goal, path and target.
_Avoid_: quarter, sprint, cycle

**Path**: How a season earns proof. A shows results you already have; B is a volume sprint to earn them.
_Avoid_: track, mode

**Channel**: Where a brand posts, such as X or a newsletter.
_Avoid_: platform, network

**Account**: A brand's account on a channel, with its persona.
_Avoid_: profile, handle

**Peer**: Someone an account learns from.
_Avoid_: influencer, competitor, follow
