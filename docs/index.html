Brand · user guide

Define and maintain many brands in one place

Brand holds everything that makes a brand itself: who it is, what it believes, how it sounds and looks, and the plan it runs by. Your agents read the same brand over MCP, so every draft, page and post starts from it.

Brands

One sidebar, every brand

Each brand sits in the sidebar with its picture, or an icon and colour, and a dot that is orange until its plan reaches 100% and green after. Open one and it shows What you believe, then the plan for this season: the Overview and the five steps, each with its own dot. The Brands page adds a brand, hides one from the sidebar, archives it or, once archived, deletes it after a backup.

The plan

hoeem's five steps

Every brand runs by the method in hoeem's "I want to turn my X account into income". Each step has a "Done when" list; a step is done when every check passes, partial when some do.

donepartialnot started
  1. 1

    Proof

    Without proof, everything after is opinion. Choose path A (results you already have) or B (a 30-day volume sprint to earn them). Proof counts when it is firsthand and has a link anyone can open.

    Done when: a path is chosen, and the season's proof links are in place.

  2. 2

    Your person

    Who it's for, their pain and what they already pay for, scored on four tests: growing market, acute pain, purchasing power and algorithmic clarity. The funnel narrows from everyone to your person.

    Done when: who, pain and pays-for are written, all four tests are scored 1 to 10, and audiences and the audience promise are set.

  3. 3

    Association

    What people learn to expect through repetition: your themes, what you stand against, your visual constants and your verbal style.

    Done when: 5 themes with a post idea, 3 things you stand against with your standard, 2 or 3 visual constants, and a verbal style.

  4. 4

    Product

    Marketing says what you promise; the free thing proves it. Each free product has a hook, a link and release gates.

    Done when: a free product has its hook, and it delivers on it.

  5. 5

    Belt

    A public project with a result, a date and visible progress, so people come back every week.

    Done when: project, exact promise, target number, launch date, where the deadline shows, the delay plan and a weekly check-in day.

Each step opens with hoeem's line and a link to his guide, with his diagrams beside it when they are in data/brand-plan-images, then the "Done when" checks and the step's own panels. Every save of the plan is a new numbered version. The Overview shows the season, today's day of 90, the goal, the path, all five steps and the next thing to do.

Agents

The same brand, over MCP

Every change in Brand is an Action, served to the page and to agents alike. To connect an agent, ask from a terminal and approve it on the Connections page:

npm run connect -- "Claude Code"

It prints the MCP endpoint and a key once. Agents read /skill.md for how to use Brand: read a brand with brand__get-entity before writing in its voice, and save plan steps with brand__save-plan-level.

Your data

Brand is open source; your brands are not

Everything a brand holds lives in data/pglite on your computer. Git ignores data/, and the pre-commit hook and npm run check:data refuse database files and .env. Brand answers only requests from the same computer.

Run it

Install and start

npm install
npm run build
npm start

It runs at http://127.0.0.1:8093. Set PORT, HOST and DATA_DIR to change the defaults. To bring brands over from Marketing, point the migration at a backup copy of its data/pglite:

npm run migrate:marketing -- --from <backup>/data/pglite
Brand is built on open-agent-kit and @jaetask/toolkit. The plan follows hoeem's method. MIT licence.
