User guide · Brand · built on open-agent-kit

Define and maintain your brands in one place

Brand holds everything that makes each of your brands itself: who it is, what it believes, how it sounds and looks, and the plan it runs by. Your agents read the same brand, so every draft and post they make starts from it.

Many brands, one appYourself, a product, a company: each brand has its own profile, lines, voice and plan.
Run by one methodEvery brand follows the five steps of hoeem's "I want to turn my X account into income", 90 days at a time.
Your data stays yoursBrand is open source. Your brands live in a folder on your computer and never go into the code.
1 · Words

The words Brand uses

Brand uses a handful of words with one meaning each. They appear on the page, in your agents' answers and in this guide.

2 · Your brands

Every brand sits in the sidebar

Each brand has a row in the sidebar with its picture, or an icon and colour, and a dot: orange while its plan is under 100%, green once every step is done. Open a brand and it shows What you believe, then this season's plan: the Overview and the five steps, each with its own dot.

The Brands page adds a brand, hides one from the sidebar, or archives it. An archived brand keeps everything. Deleting is a separate, deliberate step: you type the brand's name, and Brand writes a backup of every row before it removes anything.

3 · Profile

Who the brand is, how it sounds, and where it posts

Open the profile from the brand's row. Each panel has an Edit button that opens its own edit page with Back, Cancel and Save, so you always change one thing at a time.

4 · Lines

Lines that define the brand's character

A line is a short, quotable sentence the brand stands by, with credit when the idea is someone else's, the thought behind it and the themes it supports. Agents can suggest lines. Only you accept or reject them, and every version of a line is kept.

5 · The plan

Five steps, in hoeem's order

Every brand runs by the method in hoeem's "I want to turn my X account into income". Each step opens with hoeem's line and a link to his guide, then a Done when panel: the checks the step needs. A step is done when every check passes, partial when some do, and empty before you start.

Every save of the plan is a new numbered version, so nothing you wrote is lost. The Overview shows the season, today's day of 90, the goal, the path, all five steps and the next thing to do.

6 · Proof

Proof counts once anyone can open it

Proof is something the brand can show: something you built or did, a story, an opinion, or someone else's source. When content is easy to make, real work is what people trust, so Brand never invents a result.

An entry counts for step 1 when it is your own, firsthand, and has a link anyone can open, such as a public repo, a demo or a post. Counted entries fill the season's slots. The rest wait under Needs a link until they have one.

7 · Seasons

The plan runs 90 days at a time

A season has its dates, a one-line goal, a target number and a path. Path A shows results you already have. Path B is a volume sprint for 30 days to earn them. You choose the path once a season, and you can keep ideas for seasons you haven't planned yet.

8 · Your agents

Each agent connects once, with your OK

Everything you can do on the page is also a tool your agents can use over MCP. An agent asks to connect from a terminal, and you approve it on the Connections page, choosing whether it may only read or also change things. You can revoke it at any time.

Agents read /skill.md to learn how to use Brand. They read a brand before writing in its voice, suggest lines, add proof and save plan steps, and every change shows who made it.

9 · Running it

One app, one data folder

Brand runs on your own computer and answers only requests from it. All of its data lives in one folder, so backing it up means copying that folder. Git ignores the folder, and a check refuses any commit that contains data, so your brands never go into the code.

# install and start
npm install
npm run build
npm start      # http://127.0.0.1:8093

# bring brands over from a Marketing backup
npm run migrate:marketing -- --from <backup>/data/pglite
